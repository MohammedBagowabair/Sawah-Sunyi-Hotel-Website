The quiet field

Sunyi means quiet. We took the name seriously.

No lobby music, no loud pool deck. Just open pavilions above the subak, the thousand-year-old Balinese way of sharing water between fields, and a team from the village who know every path through it.

Rituals

Days shaped by the village

  1. 01

    Morning canang

    Fold palm-leaf offerings with the women of the house before breakfast.

  2. 02

    Temple at dawn

    A guided walk to a water temple, in a borrowed sarong, before the crowds.

  3. 03

    Ridge walk

    Two easy hours along the Campuhan ridge, ending with young coconut.

The table

Cooked over coconut husk

Our kitchen cooks what the field and the market bring in: bebek betutu wrapped in banana leaf, fern-tip urab, sambal matah pounded to order. Breakfast is served on your veranda, at any hour you like.
